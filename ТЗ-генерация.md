## Context

Untesis is a personal library of views: people answer questions and watch how their views change over time.
The landing page is not a sales page. It is a short visual story told through scrolling, in a strict black-and-white
copperplate-engraving style, with living ink dots interacting with the engravings.

The site uses two materials:

- **Engraving (E)** — placed on the page as is. Represents the world: things that existed before you and will remain.
- **Dot source (D)** — converted by code into thousands of ink dots that swirl, flow and re-form into other shapes.
  Represents thought: what flows and changes. You are not drawing the dots — you are drawing the shape the dots will
  take, so the code needs a clean, high-contrast image to sample from.

The attached reference images (engraved clouds, an engraved hourglass, an engraved Penrose triangle) show the
target style. Match their line quality and tonality.

## Style (applies to every image)

- Black and white copperplate engraving: fine stipple and cross-hatching, soft engraved shading,
  the feel of an 18th–19th-century scientific or archaeological illustration.
- **Pure white background (#FFFFFF)**, the object isolated. Night scenes too — we invert them in code.
- No color, no sepia, no paper texture, no frames or borders, no text or letters, no signatures, no watermarks,
  no cast shadows or ground on the background, no gradients, not photorealistic, not a 3D render.
- The whole object fits in frame — nothing cropped. Margin size does not matter.

## Two image types

| | Engraving (E) | Dot source (D) |
|---|---|---|
| Detail | Maximum: fine hatching welcome | Medium: fine hatching not needed |
| Priority | Beauty of the line | **Clear silhouette and strong light/shade modelling** |
| Background | Pure white | Pure white, absolutely nothing else |

5. Pairs: is it unmistakably the same object?
6. Constellation figures: is the overall pose close to the layout in the table? (The exact fit is done in code.)
